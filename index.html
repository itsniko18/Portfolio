The cards consist of Q/A and information about menstrual health, as well as coming-of-age prompts to act as cushioning questions to make them laugh and get comfortable, and to hold their attention long enough, for when the real questions arrive.

How to Play
Set Up: Stack the 5 types of cards separately. Keep the dice and timer in the center. Start: The youngest rolls the dice first. 1=Quiz, 2=Fact, 3=Talk, 4=Action, 5=Group, 6=Player's Choice. Play: Draw a card and follow the instruction within 1 minute to earn a point. Win: The player with the most points wins!

With my group, I created 4 accordions, where the 12 care scenarios (the houses that we visited during the field visit) were merged into 4 care stories. The story was curated in such a way that it focused on unfolding the inner workings of the care system - showing how the care team works throughout the day.

The story revolves around the work that the the work of the care team, their interactions with the families of care seekers, the infrastructure and how the gram panchayat worked to support the care team. We aimed to show mainly the backstage actions and how the work was intimate and personal, and not just professional for the people giving and receiving the care.

Panchakarma Treatment Pamphlet

A pamphlet that clearly explains the Panchakarma therapy in a simple way including the protocols, purpose of each treatment, how it is done, and the oils used.

It focuses more on visuals, such as illustrations of the massage process, and uses less text so that patients can quickly understand the information without feeling overwhelmed.
